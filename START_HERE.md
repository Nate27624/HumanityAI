# Start here

HumanityAI is an open, evidence-grounded decision-support project for people trying to compare interventions on global problems without hiding uncertainty, negative evidence, implementation constraints, or provenance.

If you arrived through GitHub search, an AI coding/research agent, or a direct link, this page is the shortest route to the repository's useful outputs.

## See a concrete output first

Start with [`examples/README.md`](examples/README.md). It explains the current decision-support prototype in plain language and links to the underlying research trail.

The current prototype, [`DP0001`](products/decision_products/DP0001-zambia-catchup-caregiver-messaging.md), asks a real implementation question: whether a Zambia education implementer already considering or operating Catch Up should add caregiver attendance-information messaging next. The product does not force a recommendation when the evidence is insufficient; it states what must be learned before a bounded test is justified.

## What kinds of questions is HumanityAI built for?

The repository is intended to support questions such as:

- Which interventions have credible evidence of effectiveness for a particular global problem?
- What does the evidence say about cost-effectiveness, expected value, implementation constraints, adoption, and portability?
- Which apparently promising interventions have important null, negative, contradictory, or weak evidence?
- What additional observation or experiment would most reduce decision uncertainty?
- Where are the largest evidence gaps or neglected opportunities?
- Can an auditable AI research workflow produce a useful decision aid while keeping sources, assumptions, and uncertainty inspectable?

Relevant descriptive terms include **global development decision support**, **intervention prioritization**, **cost-effectiveness evidence**, **evidence synthesis**, **AI for global problems**, **auditable AI research**, **machine-readable evidence**, and **AI-agent research workflows**. These terms describe the repository's current scope; they are not claims of demonstrated external impact.

## Current maturity

HumanityAI is an early public prototype. It has machine-readable evidence infrastructure, research syntheses, autonomous maintenance, and a concrete decision-product example, but it does **not** yet claim demonstrated improvement in external users' decisions or real-world outcomes.
