# World Problem Map — v0.1

This is a starting taxonomy, not a ranking. Many categories overlap and causal relationships between them are part of the research problem.

## P01 — War, violence, and geopolitical conflict
Armed conflict, nuclear risk, terrorism, civil violence, interstate rivalry, failed peace processes, and the institutional conditions that make cooperation difficult.

## P02 — Poverty and material insecurity
Insufficient access to income, food, basic goods, social protection, and the material conditions needed for meaningful choice.

## P03 — Health and preventable suffering
Preventable disease, inadequate healthcare access, mental health burdens, maternal and child mortality, disability, and financial barriers to care.

## P04 — Housing and human habitat
Housing affordability, homelessness, unsafe housing, displacement, urban design, infrastructure, and access to livable communities.

## P05 — Education, knowledge, and human development
Access to education, literacy, skill development, scientific knowledge, lifelong learning, and barriers preventing people from developing their abilities.

## P06 — Energy, water, sanitation, and essential infrastructure
Electricity, clean cooking, water, sanitation, transportation, communications, and other infrastructure required for modern human capability.

## P07 — Economic opportunity, work, and abundance
Unemployment, underemployment, exploitative labor, productivity, technological unemployment, access to capital, market failures, and pathways toward reducing involuntary labor through abundance.

## P08 — Rights, liberty, governance, and institutional quality
Civil liberties, political rights, corruption, rule of law, state capacity, discrimination, access to justice, and institutional accountability.

## P09 — Inequality and unequal access to opportunity
Large differences in access to resources, institutions, technology, networks, education, political influence, and other capability-enhancing assets.

## P10 — Information integrity, trust, and coordination
Misinformation, propaganda, polarization, low institutional trust, poor scientific communication, fragmented knowledge, collective-action failures, and inability to reach workable agreements.

## P11 — Climate, biodiversity, and environmental stability
Climate change, pollution, ecosystem degradation, biodiversity loss, resource depletion, and environmental conditions that constrain present and future human freedom.

## P12 — Social connection, community, and meaning
Loneliness, social fragmentation, community decline, alienation, social exclusion, and other failures of human connection that cannot be reduced to material wealth alone.

## P13 — Science, innovation, and access to technology
Slow scientific discovery, poor research coordination, inaccessible knowledge, technological bottlenecks, unequal access to useful technologies, and weak translation of research into public benefit.

## P14 — AI and emerging-technology governance
AI safety, concentration of technological power, misuse, unequal distribution of benefits, cybersecurity, biotechnology risks, autonomous systems, and governance capable of keeping pace with technological change.

## P15 — Global catastrophic and existential risks
Pandemics, nuclear catastrophe, extreme climate scenarios, dangerous emerging technologies, asteroid impacts, systemic infrastructure collapse, and other low-frequency high-consequence threats.

## P16 — Coordination infrastructure itself
The meta-problem: humanity's inability to maintain a shared, evidence-based understanding of its problems, existing work, disagreements, priorities, and opportunities for useful contribution.

## Next research task

For every problem, create:

- measurable indicators;
- current global state;
- causal hypotheses;
- major affected populations;
- existing organizations and open-source projects;
- intervention families;
- evidence for and against interventions;
- unresolved questions;
- neglected opportunities;
- dependencies on other problems;
- plausible ways AI can contribute;
- risks of AI involvement.
