## Decision question

Given one small block of additional HumanityAI research capacity, how should the next **three domain-specific producer slots** be allocated across the currently mature P03, P06, and P07 lines?

This is **not** a claim that hypertension, electricity access, and youth employment have comparable social value on one scalar. Their outcomes, populations, time horizons, and welfare domains are not commensurate. The narrower question is where another research slot is most likely to produce a decision-ready comparison rather than another descriptive artifact.

## Recommendation

Allocate the next three domain-specific producer slots as follows, unless Worker D has a newer downstream decision that changes the opportunity cost:

1. **P03 — two slots, conditionally.** Use the first to identify one high-burden country/system with public hypertension cascade, medicine/procurement, workforce, follow-up, and control data. Use the second only if at least two delivery packages can be compared on a common control definition and horizon with credible cost and retention inputs.
2. **P06 — one slot, as a denominator-recovery / value-of-information test.** Select one high-deficit country with a public least-cost electrification plan and test whether two pathways can actually be normalized to a common realized service tier, household payment burden, subsidy/financing architecture, reliability, uptake, and persistence. If those common denominators cannot be recovered, stop instead of building a synthetic ranking.
3. **P07 — zero new producer slots for now.** Preserve the Benin result as a bounded package-design diagnostic. Reopen P07 production only when Worker D can name a concrete target population and package allocation that requires a second comparable study, verified combined-arm cost, or other specific missing decision input.

This is a **research-allocation recommendation**, not a real-world spending recommendation.

## Why P03 currently has the highest marginal decision readiness

The canonical P03 gate says the central uncertainty is no longer whether antihypertensive treatment can work. The useful unit is a delivery package that repeatedly detects, initiates, titrates, supplies, follows, and retains patients until blood pressure is controlled.

Current decision inputs already identify:

- **Outcome:** controlled patient at a defined threshold and horizon, rather than screenings completed.
- **Adoption/implementation constraints:** medicine and validated-device availability, task-sharing authority, training/supervision, compensation, refill burden, treatment intensification, and retention.
- **Effectiveness anchor:** a 2024 LMIC meta-analysis found community-based strategies increased blood-pressure control overall, while the canonical note explicitly treats the pooled effect as heterogeneous rather than portable.
- **Cost state:** economic evidence for non-physician delivery is directionally promising but too heterogeneous for a portable global cost-per-controlled-patient estimate.

That combination creates a relatively clean next research test: **can one country-level system expose two feasible delivery packages with comparable controlled-patient yield, retention, and cost inputs?** If yes, a second P03 slot can produce a bounded decision comparison. If no, the line should stop after bottleneck diagnosis.

### P03 value-of-information test

Proceed to a second P03 slot only if the first slot can recover all of the following for one target system:

1. a common blood-pressure control definition and outcome horizon;
2. medicine availability/procurement or patient price information;
3. delivery-cadre cost or compensation information;
4. follow-up/retention information;
5. treatment-intensification or protocol information; and
6. at least two realistically available delivery packages or implementation variants.

**Kill condition:** if those inputs cannot be put on a common denominator, do not create a cross-package cost-effectiveness scalar.

**Confidence:** moderate-high that P03 has the best near-term path to a decision-ready country comparison; moderate on whether public cost and retention data will be complete enough.

## Why P06 deserves one targeted slot but not a broad comparison campaign

The canonical P06 gate has already resolved an important category error: technology label and connection count are not comparable outcomes. The decision unit must specify realized service tier, affordability, financing architecture, reliability, and persistence.

Current decision inputs identify:

- **Outcome:** user reaching a defined service level, with reliability/persistence where available; not cost per nominal connection.
- **Adoption constraints:** household payment burden, PAYG terms, tariff/subsidy structure, default/disconnection, financing cost, and whether the poorest households remain excluded.
- **Cost/financing anchors:** ESMAP estimates roughly $21 billion of investment for its modeled off-grid-solar universal-access potential; IEA models materially higher affordability under cheaper capital and subsidies, while still requiring demand-side support for the poorest households.
- **Effectiveness uncertainty:** canonical E0003 finds mixed downstream welfare effects, so connection/service delivery cannot be converted mechanically into education, income, health, or climate benefits.

P06 therefore has high **denominator-recovery value** but weaker immediate ranking readiness than P03. A country case can be useful if it reveals common service-tier and payment-burden data; otherwise additional producer work risks recreating the exact scalar-ranking failure the routing gate prohibits.

### P06 value-of-information test

Use one slot to answer a binary question: **Can two realistically available electricity-access pathways in one high-deficit country be compared using the same realized service tier and horizon with public financing/adoption data?**

Minimum inputs:

1. realized service tier or sufficiently detailed service attributes;
2. tariff or household payment burden;
3. financing/subsidy architecture;
4. uptake and, ideally, default/disconnection;
5. reliability/persistence; and
6. pathway cost on a denominator compatible with the service outcome.

**Kill condition:** if the comparison depends on cost per connection while service quality or financing assumptions differ materially, stop and report the missing denominator instead of ranking modalities.

**Confidence:** high that service-tier and affordability normalization are necessary; moderate that one public country case will expose enough comparable adoption and persistence data.

## Why P07 should pause producer accumulation

The integrated Benin note now supplies an unusually informative within-study comparison: separately randomized training-only, cash-only, training-plus-cash, and control arms; long follow-up; explicit component costs; and heterogeneous effects by outcome and gender.

Current decision inputs include:

- **Outcome:** long-horizon earnings and business-performance effects, with welfare/assets and employment spillovers kept distinct.
- **Cost:** about US$1,050 per beneficiary for training and about US$629 for the cash component; the canonical note correctly refuses to derive a combined-arm cost without confirmed accounting additivity.
- **Persistence:** roughly 45 months after training / 38 months after grant at the longest follow-up, with treatment-correlated attrition retained as a limitation.
- **Adoption/implementation:** very high training take-up, decentralized delivery, coaching/home visits, transport/meals/childcare accommodations, and gender-relevant implementation details.
- **Portability:** low. The result is explicitly a study-specific package diagnostic, not evidence that training dominates grants globally.

The remaining uncertainty is therefore not “collect more P07 evidence” in the abstract. It is **which concrete package allocation needs another comparator?** Until that downstream decision exists, another P07 producer slot has lower marginal value than converting P03/P06 gates into country-level decisions.

### P07 reopen test

Reopen production only if Worker D can name at least one of:

1. a target population/program where a training-vs-capital or package-design choice is actually pending;
2. a second study with sufficiently comparable costs, horizon, implementation, and outcome definitions to change that choice;
3. a specific unresolved Benin accounting input whose resolution would materially change the package judgment; or
4. a measurable adoption/implementation question not already answered by the canonical Benin note.

**Kill condition:** do not add another ALMP study merely to increase breadth when it cannot change a named package decision.

**Confidence:** high that generic P07 accumulation should pause; moderate on how soon a sufficiently comparable downstream package question will emerge.
