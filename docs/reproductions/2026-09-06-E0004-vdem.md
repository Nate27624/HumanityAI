## Qualifications preserved

The Regimes of the World classification applies thresholds to underlying V-Dem measures and the paper explicitly identifies grey-zone cases near classification boundaries.

The 74% population share is also not a measure of uniform individual experience: it is a population-weighted descriptive statistic dominated by large countries. It should remain distinct from measures of civil liberties, rule of law, state capacity, corruption, due process, and other dimensions of `P08`.

Finally, this is descriptive baseline evidence. It does not establish the effectiveness, cost-effectiveness, or desirability of any democracy-promotion or governance intervention.
