# Licensing and third-party material

HumanityAI-authored repository material is made available under the [Apache License 2.0](LICENSE), unless a file explicitly states otherwise.

## Contributions

Under Section 5 of Apache-2.0, contributions intentionally submitted for inclusion in HumanityAI are provided under the same license unless the contributor explicitly states otherwise before incorporation. Pull requests that cannot be incorporated under the repository license should not be merged until the licensing conflict is resolved.

## External evidence and data

HumanityAI frequently records facts, citations, metadata, links, summaries, and derived values from third-party research and datasets. The repository license does **not** override copyright, database rights, terms of use, attribution requirements, or other conditions attached to those external sources.

Contributors should:

- prefer linking to and citing authoritative sources rather than copying substantial source material;
- preserve required attribution and source-license metadata when known;
- avoid importing datasets or text whose terms are incompatible with redistribution;
- distinguish HumanityAI-authored analysis/metadata from third-party material;
- treat a source being publicly reachable as different from it being freely redistributable.

If the licensing status of proposed imported material is unclear, preserve the citation/link and omit the copied material until the rights question is resolved.

## No relicensing by inference

A record appearing in `data/` does not imply that the underlying source dataset, paper, website, or organization content is licensed under Apache-2.0. HumanityAI's license applies to HumanityAI's own protectable contributions to the repository; third-party rights remain with their respective holders.
